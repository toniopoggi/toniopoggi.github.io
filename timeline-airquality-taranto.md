This timeline collects public alerts, graphs and posts on air-quality
monitoring in Taranto from 2018 to 2026. It documents a continuous effort to
make ARPA data usable: making hourly values and peaks of benzene, PM10, PM2.5
and other pollutants visible, flagging anomalous situations in near real time,
and recording changes over time.

The record starts with observations in late 2018, continues through systematic
monitoring and automated alerts, the analyses presented by PeaceLink to the
Italian Senate in 2024, and the most recent evidence. Every entry is dated and
linked to its original source. Posts retain their original language and wording.
