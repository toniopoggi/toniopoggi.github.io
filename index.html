A way of working

My best work tends to combine product judgment with the stubborn pleasure of making the complete thing actually work.

01

Stay close to the real problem.

Start with what somebody is genuinely trying to understand or accomplish, not a fashionable category.

02

Make the evidence inspectable.

A useful answer should retain a path back to its data, assumptions and transformations.

03

Build across the boundaries.

Product, code, communication, operations and design are different views of the same outcome.
